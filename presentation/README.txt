TRASH PANIC! - pitch presentation (offline)

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. No internet needed.

Keys
  Right arrow / Space / click   next slide
  Left arrow                     previous slide
  T                              play the trailer full screen (Esc to close)
  N                              show the speaker notes (what to say + timing + pitch timer)
  H                              show or hide the control bar (hidden by default)
  Home                           back to the first slide

The pitch timer (in the notes, N) starts when you leave the first slide (target 5:00).

Tip: press F11 for full screen. Keep trailer.mp4 and three.min.js next to index.html.
Opened from a folder, the phone on slide 3 shows gameplay pictures; press T for the trailer itself.
